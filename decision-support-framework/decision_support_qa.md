# Decision Support QA

Use this to check whether the work is useful before handing it to a builder or reviewer.

## Business

- Does it answer one clear business question?
- Can the user understand the main point quickly?
- Does it support a real decision or next step?
- Are the conclusions backed by visible evidence?

## Data

- Do all metrics and dimensions have a source of truth?
- Are filters, thresholds, and exclusions documented?
- Are sample-size rules clear?
- Are there duplicate or competing definitions?
- Are caveats visible where they matter?

## Design

- Does the chosen pattern match the question?
- Is the most important point easy to find?
- Are details available without overwhelming the user?
- Is the wording clear and plain?

## Interpretation

- Do the claims match what the data can prove?
- Are predictions clearly labeled as predictions?
- Are "what we see" findings written that way, without making them sound like proven causes?
- Are recommendations backed by evidence?

## Done Means

- The business question is answered.
- The data contract is clear.
- The wording is safe.
- Known limits are documented.
- Platform-specific validation is complete or clearly called out.

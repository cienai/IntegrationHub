# Executive Cards

Executive cards summarize the few metrics required to interpret the page. They are not decorative tiles.

## Use When

- The metric changes the user's interpretation of the page.
- The value can be understood without opening a table.
- The metric supports the page's main business question.

## Pattern

- Label: short business phrase.
- Value: largest text in the card.
- Context: optional small line for comparison or caveat.
- Color: use semantic positive, negative, neutral, or warning only when meaningful.

## Rules

- Limit to three to five cards per page.
- Do not repeat the same metric in multiple cards.
- Do not use cards for dimensions or long text.
- Keep card sizes consistent within a row.
- Use official model measures for card values.

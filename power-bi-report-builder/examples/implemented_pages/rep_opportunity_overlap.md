# Page Spec Example: Rep Opportunity Overlap

This example captures the main lesson from the first builder test.

The first draft followed the Strategy page shape, but it led with individual reps. The better executive story is organizational participation health first, then teams, then rep outliers as supporting evidence.

## Business Question

How healthy is opportunity participation across the sales organization?

## Audience And Decision

- Primary audience: Sales leadership and Revenue Operations.
- Decision supported: Decide whether low participation is a broad operating issue, a team-level issue, a coaching issue, or a CRM hygiene issue.
- Time-to-understand target: Overall health visible within 5 seconds.

## Narrative Hierarchy

- Lead with: Overall organizational participation health.
- Support with: Distribution of reps across participation buckets and team-level average participation.
- Drill into: Outlier representatives below threshold or materially below their team average.
- Evidence: Detailed representative-level participation table.

## Recommended Top Cards

- Average Participation
- Reps Below Threshold
- Teams Below Average
- Reps With Excellent Overlap

## Recommended Primary Visuals

- Participation bucket distribution.
- Team average participation comparison.

## Supporting Detail

- Outlier rep table.
- Full rep detail table with assigned opportunities, participated opportunities, participation percentage, gap, team, and manager.

## Rule

Do not make a named rep the executive headline unless the page spec says the page is about that rep.
